OGWA Sea Glass SQUEEZE

Portable water delivery for circumstances that have gone sideways.

Coming Soon $29 /ea.

The OGWA Sea Glass SQUEEZE is a portable bidet bottle designed around a simple observation: water is very good at washing things. Its soft-sided sea-glass body is built for controlled hand pressure, while the angled elbow lid helps place water where it is actually needed without requiring advanced bathroom geometry.

No public preorder is currently active. Status checks are recorded as product-interest signals.

Field Notes

Why This Exists

The Institute has reviewed the conventional bathroom cleanup system and identified room for additional water.

Finding 01

Water Helps

Dry paper performs useful work. Water performs a different kind of work. The two systems do not need to be enemies.

Finding 02

Angle Matters

The elbow lid exists because the human wrist has already been assigned enough responsibilities.

Finding 03

Pressure Has Limits

The goal is controlled rinsing. This is personal care equipment, not municipal infrastructure.

System Note

Clean the cleaner.

Any reusable bottle used around a toilet eventually becomes part of the hygiene system itself. The bottle, lid, douser, and protective sleeve should therefore be cleaned, dried, and stored appropriately between uses. A device does not receive permanent immunity from washing merely because its assigned job involves washing something else.

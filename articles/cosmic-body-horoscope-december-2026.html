Cycle Almanac: The Moon Is a Metaphor, Not a Calendar

Unlike a horoscope, menstrual biology offers real patterns—though cycle lengths and symptoms vary widely, and some people don't menstruate. These “moon phases” are editorial labels, not claims that menstruation follows the lunar calendar.

☾ New Moon · Menstruation

Bleeding and cramps can occur; note what is normal for you. Sudden severe pain or unusually heavy bleeding deserves medical attention.

◐ Waxing Moon · Follicular Phase

Hormonal changes may influence cervical mucus. Discharge can evolve as ovulation approaches.

◯ Full Moon · Around Ovulation

Cervical mucus is often clearer, wetter or more slippery, although not everyone notices the same pattern.

◑ Waning Moon · Luteal Phase

Progesterone rises after ovulation. Some people notice different discharge or premenstrual symptoms; others notice very little.

Monthly reality check: Menstrual and lunar cycles are not reliably synchronized. Zodiac signs don't predict infection, fertility, ovulation, microbiome composition or symptoms. Don't use this feature to time contraception or make treatment decisions.

The Celestial Equipment Advisory

The practical recommendation for December is wonderfully uncommercial: your vagina does not need a seasonal fragrance. Avoid douching and internal deodorants. Clean the external vulva gently; investigate persistent itching, burning, unusual discharge, strong odor or pain with a clinician.

Things That May Improve the Towel Situation

The Deep Towel does not require advanced equipment. It mostly requires towels that can dry, sensible laundry practices and somewhere for all that moisture to go. These are a few pieces of equipment that fit the assignment.

Equipment 01

Towels That Can Recover

A towel needs to absorb water without remaining a damp monument to yesterday's shower. A good towel should balance absorbency, comfort and the ability to dry thoroughly between uses.

Inspect the Towels →
Equipment 02

Detergent — Used Sensibly

More detergent does not automatically produce more clean. The goal is effective washing without leaving behind an unnecessary layer of detergent residue for the towel to carry into its next assignment.

Inspect the Detergent →
Equipment 03

Somewhere for the Towel to Actually Dry

This may be the least glamorous breakthrough in the entire investigation. Spread the towel out, expose more surface area to moving air and give the moisture somewhere to go.

Equipment 04

Dryer Balls

A simple option for helping separate laundry during the drying cycle without coating towels in traditional fabric softener. Less glamorous than laboratory equipment. Potentially more useful.

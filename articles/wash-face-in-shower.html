Field Report 009 · Face Systems

Should You Wash Your Face in the Shower?

Your face is attached to your body. Your body is in the shower. There is water. There is soap...

FINDING 01 — Yes. You can wash your face in the shower.

We have excellent news for operational efficiency. There is no fundamental reason a face cannot be washed...

FINDING 02 — The water is probably hotter than your face requested.

Here we encounter the real suspect. Hot showers feel fantastic...

FINDING 03 — Your pores do not open and close like little doors.

We need to deal with this. You have probably heard that hot water opens pores...

FINDING 04 — Body wash is not automatically face wash.

You are standing in the shower. Your body wash is already in your hand...

THE MEISTER'S VERDICT

Should you wash your face in the shower? Sure. Inspection status: APPROVED LOCATION

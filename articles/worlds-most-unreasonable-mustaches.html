The Mustache Files · Let's do mustache stuff.

The World's Most Unreasonable Mustaches

Fourteen feet of mustache. Decades of growth. International recordkeeping. At some point, facial hair becomes infrastructure.

Most mustaches require a comb, a trim, and occasional diplomacy with a sandwich. Others demand decades of commitment and an independent measuring authority. The Institute has opened a file.

Finding 01: Fourteen feet is a lot of mustache

Ram Singh Chauhan of India was recognized by Guinness World Records for a mustache measuring 4.29 meters (about 14 feet) in 2010. He had been growing it since 1970. He has described using oils to care for the hair, which is understandable when your upper lip has become a long-term agricultural concern.

Chauhan also appeared in the James Bond film Octopussy. The Institute considers this a reasonable career development for a man with fourteen feet of mustache.

Finding 02: The definition is doing considerable work

Guinness also recognizes Paul Slosar for the longest mustache on a living man under a more specific competitive definition: approximately 25 inches, verified in 2022. That does not mean Chauhan's longer historical achievement disappeared. The records have different definitions and categories.

Guinness has said it redefined its living-person category to align with competitive facial-hair standards. The precise date Guinness first began tracking mustache length is not established in the publicly available material we reviewed. We will not invent an inaugural mustache.

Finding 03: The upper lip is for everyone

Erin Honeycutt has held Guinness recognition for the longest mustache on a living woman, with a measurement reported at 12.2 inches in 2024. Honeycutt has discussed her experience with polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), which can cause facial-hair growth, and her decision to embrace that hair rather than remove it.

Her story is a useful reminder: facial hair is biology and personal expression, not a membership card for one gender.

Finding 04: How do you measure such a thing?

Measurement rules matter. Guinness has described wetting and combing mustache hair straight for certain record attempts. Competitive standards can also restrict which hairs count based on where they grow. A cheek hair cannot simply announce that it has joined the mustache.

Finding 05: Maintenance is not optional

Length creates practical concerns: tangles, pulling, trapped food, product buildup and damp hair against the skin. Gentle washing when needed, thorough rinsing and drying, careful detangling, and avoiding harsh traction are sensible basics. There is no evidence that every mustache requires an elaborate daily chemical program.

THE MEISTER'S VERDICT

Outstanding achievement in upper-lip development. The Institute applauds the patience and questions the soup logistics.

Field Report 008 · Face Systems

Do You Actually Need Beard Oil?

Beard oil comes in a small bottle. This is important. Small bottles allow products to become expensive...

FINDING 01 — Beard oil is mostly exactly what it sounds like.

Beard oil is oil intended for use on facial hair and the skin underneath it...

FINDING 02 — Beard oil primarily conditions hair and skin.

Facial hair can be coarse. As a beard grows longer, that coarseness becomes more noticeable...

FINDING 03 — Beard oil does not make follicles appear.

Here we must disappoint the internet. Your beard's growth pattern is influenced heavily by genetics...

FINDING 04 — Beard oil and moisturizer are not the same thing.

Traditional facial moisturizers often contain humectants, emollients, and occlusives...

THE MEISTER'S VERDICT

Do you actually need beard oil? Probably not. But you might genuinely like it. Inspection status: OPTIONAL BUT USEFUL EQUIPMENT

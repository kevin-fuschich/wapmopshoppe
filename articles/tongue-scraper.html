Field Report 002 · Mouth Systems

Do You Actually Need a Tongue Scraper?

You brush your teeth. You floss when you're being responsible. And then there's your tongue, sitting right in the middle of everything...

FINDING 01 — Your tongue has terrain.

Stick out your tongue. We'll wait. What you're looking at isn't a smooth pink slab...

FINDING 02 — Your tongue may be one of a kind.

This has almost nothing to do with whether you need a tongue scraper. We are including it because it is wonderful...

FINDING 03 — The main reason people scrape their tongues is breath.

Bad breath — halitosis, if we're putting on our laboratory coats — frequently originates in the mouth...

FINDING 04 — Scraping works. The question is how much.

Research comparing tongue cleaning methods has found that scraping or brushing the tongue can temporarily reduce tongue coating and odor-producing compounds...

FINDING 05 — You already own a tongue-cleaning device.

It's called a toothbrush. You can brush your tongue. Many people do. And it works...

FINDING 06 — There is such a thing as too much enthusiasm.

Your tongue is tissue. Not cookware. The goal is to remove loose coating from the surface...

THE MEISTER'S VERDICT

Do you actually need a tongue scraper? Need? Probably not. Worth trying? Absolutely. Inspection status: APPROVED FOR FIELD USE

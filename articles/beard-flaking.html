Why Is the Skin Under Your Beard Flaking?

You put on a black shirt. This was your first mistake. You scratch your beard. And there they are...

FINDING 01 — Your beard did not replace your skin.

This seems obvious when written down. It becomes less obvious after six months of beard growth...

FINDING 02 — Flakes do not automatically mean "dry skin."

This distinction matters. Dry skin can absolutely flake, but several conditions can produce scaling...

FINDING 03 — Seborrheic dermatitis is not evidence that you are dirty.

This deserves emphasis because dandruff has suffered from terrible public relations...

FINDING 04 — Overwashing can create its own problem.

Flakes appear. You decide the beard must be dirty. You wash it. Still flakes...

THE MEISTER'S VERDICT

Why is the skin under your beard flaking? Could be dryness, irritation, or a skin condition. Inspection status: CAUSE REQUIRES IDENTIFICATION
